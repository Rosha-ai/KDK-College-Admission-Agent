[SECTION: ELIGIBILITY_CRITERIA]
First Year B.Tech Eligibility Criteria after 12th (HSC):
1. Candidate must be an Indian National.
2. Passed HSC (10+2 / 12th standard) examination of Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education or its equivalent examination.
3. Compulsory subjects: Physics and Mathematics along with one of Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science, Information Technology, Informatics Practices, Agriculture, Engineering Graphics, or Business Studies.
4. Marks requirement: Obtained at least 45% marks (at least 40% marks in case of Backward Class categories, Economically Weaker Section (EWS), and Persons with Disability candidates belonging to Maharashtra State only) in the above subjects taken together.
5. Entrance Exam Requirement: Must have obtained a non-zero positive score in MHT-CET 2026 or JEE Main Paper-I. CET or JEE is mandatory for State CAP and All India seats.

[SECTION: ADMISSION_PROCESS]
Admission Process after 12th (HSC):
Step 1: Registration on the official Maharashtra State CET Cell portal (mahacet.org) for Centralized Admission Process (CAP).
Step 2: Document Verification and Confirmation at Scrutiny Center (E-Scrutiny or Physical Scrutiny).
Step 3: Display of Provisional and Final Merit List by State CET Cell.
Step 4: Online Option Form submission (choice filling of colleges and branches, choosing KDKCE Choice Codes).
Step 5: Seat Allotment in CAP Round I, II, and III.
Step 6: Seat acceptance payment and reporting to the allotted institute (KDKCE, Nagpur) with original documents and fees to confirm admission.
Institutional / Management Quota Seats: Conducted at the college level strictly as per DTE/CET Cell merit norms.

[SECTION: FEES_STRUCTURE]
Annual Fee Structure for Academic Year 2026-27 (Approved by Fees Regulating Authority - FRA Maharashtra):
1. Open / General Category: Approximately Rs. 98,000 to Rs. 1,08,000 per year (Tuition Fee + Development Fee + University exam & other charges).
2. OBC / EBC / EWS Category: Approximately 50% Tuition Fee concession (Student pays around Rs. 54,000 to Rs. 60,000 per year).
3. VJNT / SBC / TFWS Category: Tuition Fee is 100% exempted; student pays only Development Fee and miscellaneous charges (Approximately Rs. 12,000 to Rs. 16,000 per year).
4. SC / ST Category: 100% tuition and development fee exemption under Government of Maharashtra scholarship schemes (Student pays approximately Rs. 2,500 to Rs. 4,500 exam/processing charges).
Hostel Fees: Separate boys and girls hostel facility is available near campus with fees ranging from Rs. 40,000 to Rs. 55,000 per year including mess/accommodation.
